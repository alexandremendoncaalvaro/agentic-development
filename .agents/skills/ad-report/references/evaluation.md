# Report evaluation

## Invariant ledger

Create an internal invariant ledger containing every claim, name, number, date,
citation, URL, identifier, commitment, uncertainty, exclusion, and requested
action from the evidence and brief. Confirm each remains unchanged or was
explicitly corrected by the user. A renderer or voice pass cannot weaken this
ledger.

## Editorial and template coverage

Account for every applicable template rule and selected exemplar strength:

- `satisfied`: present and adequate;
- `improved`: retained with a named audience- or target-specific improvement;
- `not applicable`: irrelevant, with a concrete reason;
- `higher-priority override`: displaced by a factual, safety, evidence, or format
  hard constraint, with the constraint named.

Verify that the executive conclusion is self-contained, findings are traceable to
method and evidence provenance, conclusions do not outrun findings,
recommendations follow from conclusions, and limitations are visible where they
affect use.

## Artifact verification

Record the composed capability and its render receipt. Inspect every page, slide,
chart, or responsive state required by that capability. Check headings, reading
order, labels, alt text or textual equivalents, contrast, table structure, link
meaning, and that no content is clipped, blank, or overlapping. Do not claim a
visual or accessibility pass when the required tool did not run.

## Comparison protocol

"Same or better" requires traceable coverage of every applicable strength; strict
preference additionally needs a named target-specific improvement. Compare held-out
briefs not used to derive the template, preserve invariant ledgers, randomize
presentation order, and ask the owner to judge usefulness. Retain only aggregate
ratings and non-private rationales in repository artifacts.
