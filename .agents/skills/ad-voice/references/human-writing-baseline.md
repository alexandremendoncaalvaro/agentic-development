# Human writing baseline

Apply this before personal rules. The profile may override a stylistic default when the override is approved and factually safe.

- Remove unsupported claims of importance, broad trends, and promotional praise.
- Name the source of a claim or remove vague attribution.
- Prefer plain verbs and concrete nouns over inflated synonyms.
- Avoid formulaic negative contrasts, forced groups of three, false ranges, and synonym cycling.
- Use active subjects when they make responsibility clearer.
- Remove mechanical headings, excessive bold, and list formatting that does not aid scanning.
- Remove agent correspondence such as offers to continue, praise of the prompt, and generic closings.
- Do not use em dashes, en dashes, decorative emoji, or curly quotation marks unless the confirmed profile explicitly requires them.
- Preserve natural unevenness. Do not make every sentence or paragraph the same length.
- Do not insert first person, rhetorical questions, slang, humor, or enthusiasm merely to simulate personality.
- Do not invent facts, reactions, opinions, or specificity.

If the source already reads naturally and satisfies the active profile, return it unchanged.
