# Publication evaluation

## Source-role ledger

Classify each supplied item as target-thread context, intended outward content,
supporting evidence, private deliberation, or a constraint before drafting. The
roles control inclusion:

- intended outward content is eligible for the publication;
- target-thread context establishes what recipients already know;
- supporting evidence verifies or qualifies claims;
- private deliberation stays out unless the owner explicitly promotes it;
- constraints govern the result but are not workflow narration.

Context and evidence do not become publication content automatically. When a role
is ambiguous, exclude it unless omission would prevent an honest answer.

## Invariant ledger

Build an internal invariant ledger from intended outward content in the brief or
original draft: every claim, name, number, date, citation, URL, identifier,
commitment, uncertainty, exclusion, and requested action. Before returning,
confirm that each remains unchanged or was explicitly corrected by the user. Do
not trade factual preservation for fluency or mistake background for content.

## Reply relevance ledger

For a collaboration reply, identify the unresolved question or requested action,
the direct answer, and each net-new fact the recipients need next. Remove any
sentence that only restates settled thread context, exposes private deliberation,
or explains a term the active participants have already demonstrated they
understand. Retain repetition only for a necessary correction, contrast, safety
warning, or explicitly requested recap.

Reject an opener that merely labels what follows as a caveat, context, topic, or
additional point. A short social bridge is acceptable only when the same sentence
delivers the concrete answer or delta. For each mechanism-level explanation, ask
whether it changes what the recipient should understand, decide, or do next; if
not, keep it as private supporting evidence and state only the practical
conclusion.

## Coverage ledger

Account for every applicable template rule and selected exemplar strength with one
verdict:

- `satisfied`: present and adequate;
- `improved`: preserved with a named target-specific improvement;
- `not applicable`: irrelevant to this publication, with a concrete reason;
- `higher-priority override`: displaced by a factual, safety, or destination hard
  constraint, with that constraint named.

Revise any material item without a defensible verdict. A remaining material gap is
reported outside the usable publication.

## Comparison protocol

"Same or better" is not a popularity or length score. It requires traceable
coverage of every applicable strength plus at least one named improvement for a
strict-preference claim. Compare held-out prompts not used to derive the baseline,
preserve their invariant ledgers, and randomize presentation order when asking the
owner to compare templated and untemplated results. Record only aggregate ratings
and non-private rationales in repository artifacts.
