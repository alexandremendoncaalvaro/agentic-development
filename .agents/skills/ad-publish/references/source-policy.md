# Publication source policy

## Access boundary

The resolved template is approved durable state and may be read directly. Before
reading any additional private example, obtain approval for the exact service or
path, repository, channel or document set, author filter and author class, relevant
date range, intended publication type, and retention mode.

A public URL supplied by the user authorizes only that URL. Existing credentials,
connectors, or repository access do not authorize adjacent searches. If a source
or connector is unavailable, continue from supplied text, approved local files,
public owner-supplied URLs, or the bundled baseline. State the limitation outside
the publication when material. The workflow does not fabricate prior owner or team
patterns.

## Selection

Match artifact type, destination, audience, purpose, and use conditions. Select the
smallest evidence set that adds a load-bearing strength not already represented by
the template. Recency, length, reactions, and popularity may discover a candidate
but do not establish usefulness.

Keep owner, community, external, and official authorship separate. Community
evidence may establish shared conventions but never becomes owner identity or a
license to copy distinctive phrasing.

## Retention

Normal drafting is read-only. Do not retain source bodies, exports, or new derived
rules. A request to preserve a reusable improvement routes explicitly to
`ad-template-tune`, including its source authorization, exact-excerpt retention,
one-delta, and write-approval gates.
